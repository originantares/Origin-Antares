Our Educational Mission

We believe the future of India's space programme lives in classrooms and college labs today. Through structured programmes, real hardware, and direct industry exposure, we bridge the gap between academic learning and professional space engineering.

Our Programmes

CubeSat Academy

Undergraduate6 months30 per cohort

End-to-end CubeSat design, build, and test programme. Students deliver a flight-ready 1U CubeSat under mentor guidance.

Satellite Systems Bootcamp

Postgraduate / Industry4 weeks20 per cohort

Intensive hands-on bootcamp covering ADCS, EPS, OBC, and propulsion integration on real flight hardware.

Research Internship

Final Year / Masters3–6 months10 per cohort

Embedded research internship within Origin Antares engineering teams working on live satellite programmes.

Space Policy & Law
