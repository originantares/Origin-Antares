Satellite Bus Platforms

Three Bus Platforms. Infinite Configurations.

Our modular bus architecture supports a wide range of mission profiles, from small CubeSat-class platforms to full-size GEO spacecraft.

CubeSat / NanoSat

OA-Nano

Ultra-compact platform designed for rapid deployment and constellation missions. Standardised 3U–16U form factor with modular payload bays and commercial off-the-shelf components for cost efficiency.

MicroSat

OA-Micro

Mid-class workhorse platform for Earth observation, communications, and technology demonstration. Features redundant avionics, high-agility ADCS, and a flexible payload interface supporting optical, SAR, and RF payloads.

MASS RANGE50 – 200 kg
POWER200 – 600 W
ORBITLEO / MEO / SSO
DESIGN LIFE5 – 8 years
Full-Size Spacecraft

OA-Prime

High-performance platform for demanding commercial and defense missions. Radiation-hardened subsystems, high-throughput Ka/Ku-band communications, and advanced thermal management for extended GEO operations.

MASS RANGE300 – 1200 kg
POWER1 – 5 kW
ORBITLEO / GEO / GTO
DESIGN LIFE10 – 15 years
