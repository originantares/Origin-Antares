What is STELLARSAT?

Redefining Micro, Small & Medium Scale Cube Satellites

Satellite for Tracking and Earth Low Level Analysis and Reporting

STELLARSAT is the flagship commercial CubeSat line developed by Origin Antares® Incorp., engineered to empower Industries, Researchers, Businesses, Farming ecosystems and so on.. with highly efficient, scalable, and intelligent space-borne systems. Designed for versatility and mass-manufacturing, STELLARSAT represents a new era where access to space is simpler, faster, and more affordable without compromising performance.

Built with precision and innovation at Origin Antares® state-of-the-art manufacturing, STELLARSAT combines high-performance electronics, modular payload bays, and robust communication systems to support missions from Earth observation to climate monitoring, IoT connectivity, deep-space data collection, and Farming-grade intelligence.

A Next-Generation Commercial CubeSat Built for the World

STELLARSAT, developed by Origin Antares® Incorp., is a cutting edge CubeSat platform specifically designed for micro, small, and medium-scale satellite missions. Engineered with the philosophy of "Space for Everyone", each STELLARSAT unit is built to deliver high reliability at an accessible cost, unlocking opportunities across commercial, academic, industrial, and governmental sectors.

Mass-Manufactured for Scalability

STELLARSAT is developed using a high-precision, standardized mass-manufacturing process, enabling faster production cycles, lower mission costs, and the ability to deploy satellite fleets or constellations efficiently.

Smart On-Board Computing (OBC)

STELLARSAT integrates next-gen processing units including STM32 microcontrollers, Raspberry Pi-based microcomputing units, and custom flight-computer architecture (OBC-STL series) — enabling real-time data processing, fault tolerance, onboard decision-making, and advanced telemetry handling.

Designed for Affordability and Accessibility

STELLARSAT helps businesses and institutions enter the space domain without the high financial barrier. Its reduced deployment cost and streamlined launch integration make it ideal for both individuals and large enterprises.

Global Commercial Reach

STELLARSAT is engineered to serve global customers with high reliability, mission assurance, and long-term support.

Platform Features

Modular Payload Interface

Standardised MIL-STD-1553 and SpaceWire interfaces allow rapid payload swap without bus redesign.

Propulsion-Ready

Integrated cold-gas and electric propulsion mounts for orbit raising, station-keeping, and deorbit.

Radiation-Hardened Core

TID-rated avionics and SEU-tolerant memory for multi-year LEO and MEO operations.

Autonomous Operations

Onboard AI-driven fault detection and recovery reduces ground contact requirements by 60%.

Rapid Integration

AIT-ready design with standardised harness and panel interfaces cuts integration time to under 6 weeks.

Secure Telemetry

End-to-end encrypted TT&C with frequency-hopping spread spectrum for anti-jam resilience.
