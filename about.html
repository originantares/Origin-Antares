Our Story

From a Shared Vision to the Stars

Origin Antares was founded by a team of engineers and scientists who believed that access to space should not be limited by geography or budget. Born in India, we set out to build satellite systems that are reliable, affordable, and mission-ready for the world's most demanding applications.

From our early days designing CubeSat bus platforms to delivering full-stack satellite solutions, every milestone has been driven by one conviction: that India's engineering talent can lead the next era of space exploration and utilisation.

Today we work with government agencies, research institutions, and commercial operators across the globe — bringing precision-engineered spacecraft from concept to orbit.
